# RELEASE v0.5.0: A normal desktop app that floats

**Status:** active
**Exit gate:** the app shows the Big Blue Fish icon in the Dock and the menu bar, and the pet floats above other windows on every Space
**Depends on:** v0.4.1
**Requirements:** R-W2, R-I1

## Scope

Two features, both requested after 0.4.0:

1. **Dock and menu-bar presence.** `ActivationPolicy::Regular` replaces `Accessory`, so the installed `DeepseekPet.app` appears in the Dock and Cmd+Tab with the Big Blue Fish icon. A menu-bar icon (its own 44 px `icons/tray.png`, full colour, not template-tinted) offers "Show Big Blue Fish" and "Quit". Clicking the Dock icon restores a pet hidden with "Hide for now" (`RunEvent::Reopen`). This supersedes the v0.1.0 criterion "not in Dock". Cmd+Q quits through the normal app menu.
2. **Always floating, like a desktop pet.** The window keeps `alwaysOnTop` and `visibleOnAllWorkspaces`, transparent and borderless, so it follows you across Spaces and stays above normal windows. Making the app Regular must not lower that level; a test guards it.

Out of scope: floating over full-screen apps (needs a different window level and collection behaviour), a setting to switch Dock visibility off.
