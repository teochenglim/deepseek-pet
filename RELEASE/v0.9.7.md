# RELEASE v0.9.7: Show Claude context is a floating window of its own, Show Big Blue Fish always redraws the pet

**Status:** active
**Exit gate:** after Show Claude context, a played clip and Show Big Blue Fish in any order the pet is drawn; the context window floats on its own; `make check` is green
**Depends on:** v0.9.6
**Requirements:** R-X1

No new dependency.

## What failed

- After Show Claude context and Play animation, **Show Big Blue Fish made the pet disappear.** The pet's page held state (a note layout, a played clip, a stopped animator) that the 0.9.6 reset tried to undo piece by piece.
- The context note shared the pet's page and window (speech bubble, window growing), which was "buggy" in 0.9.4 to 0.9.6.

## Fix

- **Show Claude context is independent.** `context_window::open` creates a small borderless transparent always-on-top window (`context-N`, page `src/context.html`) next to the pet: above it with the right edges aligned, below it when there is no room above, inside the screen. It fades over its last two seconds and closes after 20 s (click it to close sooner; opening it again replaces it). The pet's page and window are never touched. Removed: the note mode in the bubble, the window growth (`note_window.rs`), the `--sprite-top` layout, the `show-context`/`reset-pet` events. The capability `context.json` lets only the note window close itself.
- **Show Big Blue Fish is unconditional.** `commands::show_pet_fresh` shows the window and **reloads the pet's page**, so the pet is drawn from scratch whatever state it was in. The window keeps its place: it is remembered before the reload (only if its centre is on a screen) and the reloaded page asks for it back (`apply_restore_position`) after its own default positioning; an off-screen pet therefore comes back in the default corner. The Dock click does the same.

## Limits

- Not seen on screen by the agent. Manual pass: Show Claude context from the menu-bar icon and the right-click menu (a separate small window appears next to the pet, the pet does not move, it fades and closes after 20 s, click closes it); then Play animation, Show Claude context, Show Big Blue Fish in every order, also after Hide for now and after dragging the pet near a screen edge: the pet is always drawn.
- The reload restarts the pet's page: the welcome bubble reappears and the current animation restarts. A pet that was on screen stays where it was; there may be a brief flash while the page lays itself out.
- The note window's height is estimated from the number of text lines; an unusually long model name could clip a line.
- The window does not take focus (`focused(false)`) but macOS may still raise the app; not verified.
