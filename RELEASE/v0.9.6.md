# RELEASE v0.9.6: the pet never disappears after Show Claude context, Show Big Blue Fish redraws it, the note fades after 20 s

**Status:** active
**Exit gate:** after Show Claude context the pet is still drawn where it was, "Show Big Blue Fish" always brings it back, the note leaves after 20 seconds, and `make check` is green
**Depends on:** v0.9.5
**Requirements:** R-X1

No new dependency.


> **Superseded in v0.9.7:** the note no longer lives in the pet's speech bubble or grows the pet window; Show Claude context is a floating window of its own (`context_window.rs`), and Show Big Blue Fish reloads the pet's page. The acceptance criteria below that described the bubble and the window growth were dropped or reworded.

## What

- **The pet cannot be pushed out any more.** In 0.9.4/0.9.5 the speech bubble sat in the page flow above the pet, so its height decided where the pet was drawn, and a tall note (or a window that could not grow) left the pet outside the window. Now the note bubble is laid over the room the window grew into (`position: absolute`) and the pet's distance from the top is set explicitly (`--sprite-top` = where it was + how much the window grew). If the window cannot grow, the growth is 0, the pet stays where it was and the note overlays the room above it.
- **Show Big Blue Fish always renders the pet** (menu-bar icon, and the Dock click). `commands::show_pet_fresh` shows the window, undoes any note growth, moves a window that is off every screen to the primary screen's bottom-right corner (`note_window::reset`), and emits `reset-pet`; the page ends any note, clears its layout, resets the pet's visibility and size, and restarts the animator so a frame is drawn.
- **The note lasts 20 seconds** (`context::NOTE_MS`, sent to the page in the event) and fades out over the last two.

## Limits

- The page half (CSS layout, the redraw on `reset-pet`, the fade) is not covered by Rust tests and was not seen on screen by the agent. Manual pass: Show Claude context from both menus, pet stays put and visible; wait 20 s (fade, window back to normal size); click Show Big Blue Fish after Hide for now and after dragging the pet off a screen edge.
- If the window cannot grow, the note overlays the top of the pet window and can cover part of the pet until it fades; the pet is still drawn.
- A pet whose window is only partly off screen (centre still on a screen) is not moved.
